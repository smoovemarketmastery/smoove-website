Accessibility
Statement

Last updated: November 2026  ·  $moove Market Mastery

1. Our Commitment

We want everyone to be able to use the $moove Market Mastery website, including people with disabilities. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA.

2. What We Have Done

Measures we have taken include:

  • Readable text sizes and color contrast that meet WCAG AA
  • Full keyboard navigation with visible focus and a "Skip to content" link
  • Descriptive labels for buttons and controls
  • Touch targets sized for phones
  • Respect for your device's reduced-motion setting: the animated background shows a still frame instead
  • Layouts that work on phones, tablets and desktops

3. Known Limitations

Checkout, membership, the community and the indicators run on third-party platforms (Whop, Discord and TradingView), and we do not control their accessibility. The product screenshots in the showcase are images; the text beside each one describes what it shows.

4. Feedback

If you have trouble using any part of the website, email us at SmooveMarketMastery@gmail.com and tell us the page and the problem. We aim to respond within five business days and can provide information in another format.
