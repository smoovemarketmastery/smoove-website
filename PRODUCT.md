# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Intermediate futures traders who find $moove on social media (TikTok, Instagram, X). They arrive from a bio link, mostly on a phone, already past the basics and tired of losing trades they can't explain. They decide quickly whether this is worth a monthly payment.

## Product Purpose

$moove Market Mastery sells a trading education system built for NQ and ES futures, with principles that carry over to FX and metals. The site's job is to turn a social visitor into a paying member of one of two plans. Success is a completed Whop checkout.

## Positioning

The indicators were built for NQ and ES from the start, around one methodology (AMDX / Quarterly Theory, an ICT-style price delivery framework). The five indicators, the course and the community all teach and apply that same framework. The EA bot is a gated next step for active members, not a product anyone can buy.

## Operating Context

- Checkout and membership run on Whop. Course content lives in the Whop dashboard.
- Indicators are invite-only Pine Script tools on TradingView. Access is added by hand to the buyer's TradingView username within 24 hours.
- The community is a Discord server. Access is granted automatically by the Whop bot.
- The EA bot runs on MetaTrader 5 and trades XAUUSD (gold). Access is by application, requires an active membership, MT5 and a $5,000 minimum deposit.
- The site is a static page on GitHub Pages at smoovemarketmastery.github.io/smoove-website. There is no custom domain yet.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Indicators plan:** $124.99 a month or $1,099 a year. All five TradingView indicators.
- **Full Program:** $247 a month or $2,197 a year. Indicators plus the course (6 chapters, 18 lessons) and the full community.
- **Indicators:** Smoove AMDX, Smoove PO3, Smoove Inducement Macros, Smoove QT SSMT and Smoove QT + Macros.
- **Billing:** monthly plans cancel anytime and keep access to the end of the period. All sales are final, with no refunds.
- **Audience level:** the program is for intermediate to advanced traders, not beginners.
- **Contact:** SmooveMarketMastery@gmail.com, assembled in the browser so scrapers miss it.
- Regulatory and legal pages (risk, earnings and CFTC disclaimers, terms, privacy, cookies) are required and stay linked from every page.

## Brand Commitments

- The name is written "$moove Market Mastery", with a dollar sign.
- **Faceless brand:** $moove speaks as a brand. There is no founder name, photo or first-person founder story.
- Direct, confident trader voice aimed at serious traders ("This isn't for everyone").

## Evidence on Hand

None verifiable yet. There are no member testimonials with permission, no verified trading results, no stated member count and no press. The earlier testimonials section was removed on 2026-11-07 because its quotes couldn't be verified.

Future work must not invent or imply any of these:

- testimonials, reviews or reactions
- profit, win-rate or performance figures
- member or community counts
- "limited spots" scarcity
- activity claims such as "24/7" or "every major financial hub"

The product sells on what it concretely includes. Real member quotes, collected with permission, may be added later.

## Product Principles

1. **Sell the system, never the outcome.** Describe what a member gets and how it works. Make no promise about results, which keeps it honest and inside the disclaimers.
2. **Clarity before hype.** A phone visitor from social should understand the two plans, the price and how access works within seconds.
3. **One framework, three tools.** Indicators, course and community are one AMDX / QT system, not a bundle of extras.
4. **Gated, not sold.** The EA bot is earned through membership and application. It is never pitched as a purchasable shortcut to profit.
5. **Plain about the friction.** Manual TradingView access, the 24-hour wait and the no-refunds policy are stated up front.
